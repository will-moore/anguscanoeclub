## Welcome

Welcome to **Angus Canoe Club**! Whether you've never held a paddle or you've been on the water for years, you'll find a friendly crowd here. We run relaxed *beginner sessions*, social paddles and day trips throughout the year, and all the kit you need to get started is provided.

Come along, meet the crew, and discover your local waterways.
